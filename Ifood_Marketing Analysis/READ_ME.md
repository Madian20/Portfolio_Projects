## Project Overview

**IFood Marketing Analysis** is an end-to-end data analytics project built on a real-world marketing dataset from Maven Analytics, covering **2,240 customers** across **25 fields** — including customer profiles, product spending, purchase channels, and responses to 5 marketing campaigns.

The goal is to understand what drives customer engagement, identify the most effective campaigns and channels, and profile the typical Maven Marketing customer.

The project kicked off in **SQL Server** with building the database and core tables, followed by a **data cleaning phase** to ensure the data is reliable and ready for analysis. Calculated columns were then added to derive meaningful metrics from the existing fields, and **five dedicated views** were created to make the data easier to query and explore across different analytical angles.

With the data fully prepared, an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** was conducted in **Python** to surface patterns and relationships across customer segments. The project concludes with an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that brings all findings together in a clear, decision-ready format.
